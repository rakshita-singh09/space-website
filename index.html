The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite, a rocky, cratered body orbiting our planet at an average distance of 238,855 miles. As the fifth-largest satellite in the solar system, it is roughly one-quarter the size of Earth, lacks a breathable atmosphere, and reflects sunlight, causing its changing phases. [1, 2, 3, 4]
